What it does

Quote → job → invoice is one click at each step, and each conversion is refused twice, so the same job can't be invoiced two ways.

Quotes

Numbered per business, per-line GST toggle, a clean PDF, one click to turn an accepted quote into a job.

Jobs

A diary by scheduled date, status through to invoiced, one click to an invoice with the lines already on it.

Tax invoices

10% GST, part payments, overdue tracking, PDF. The ABN and tax-invoice status are frozen at issue.

Payments

Paid, part-paid and overdue are derived from money received — never typed in, so the dashboard can't lie to you.

Dashboard

Outstanding, overdue, received, quotes out, twelve months of receipts, and what needs attention today.

Your data

The GST detail that costs people money

GST is rounded to cents at each line and then summed — not calculated on the total. The two disagree by a cent often enough to start an argument, and the ATO expects the former. If you're not registered for GST, you charge none, whatever a line says.

Installing it (60 seconds)

  1. Open the .dmg and drag TradeFlow into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software — the beta isn't notarised by Apple yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press Open Anyway. (Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TradeFlow.app.)
  3. Paste your licence key when the app asks for it. That's the whole setup.
  4. Settings → Data → Load the example gives you a fictional business to poke at before you put real work in.

Apple silicon Macs (M1 and later), macOS 13 Ventura or newer. An Intel build is on the list if beta users need one — say so in the feedback box.
